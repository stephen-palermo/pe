The PE Nimination Committee is using these Technical Readiness Indicators (TRIs) to judge candidate Readiness:

(1) Expertise-What you know
(1a) Top domain expert and trusted advisor recognized by Intel, customers, partners, and industry
(1b) Solves complex technical problems with clear, actionable solutions anchored in customer requirements and industry ecosystems
(1c) Exercises expert judgment and decision making to evaluate trade-offs and reduce complexity in solution development based on expertise of emerging trends, customer needs, and strategic business priorities
(1d) Builds, sustains, and leverage networks across Intel and industry to drive technology development
(1e) Models continuous learning and applies outside-in thinking to advance breadth and depth of technical expertise

(2) Leadership-How you lead
(2a) Sets clear technical direction anchored in customer success and drives execution aligned with organizational goals
(2b) Models accountability, owns outcomes, admits mistakes, and learns from failure
(2c) Invites, listens, and acts on customer feedback with humility and transparency
(2d) Develops technical talent and the technologist community; mentors and grows others
(2e) Steward of Intel’s technology-driven culture; builds trust and followership through innovation and empowerment of others’ success 
(2f) Acts as a change agent and effectively influences across Intel and the industry in pursuit of advancing technology and product development 

(3) Impact-Results you achieve
(3a) Demonstrates a track record of relentless execution in bringing products and technologies to market
(3b) Achieves quantifiable and sustained results aligned to organizational goals and Intel’s business outcomes
(3c) Prioritizes and actively solves Intel’s most critical and complex technical problems 
(3d) Formulates Intel’s technology strategy and delivers breakthrough leadership solutions for internal and external customers 
(3e) Delivers predictable, on time, and quality results that consistently beat competitors 
(3f) Shapes the technology ecosystems, influences industry standards, and achieves meaningful impact in the markets where Intel compete
